## Rulings

| question | ruling |

## Fences

- No new image generation. Crops and trims only.
- Story text on the site does not change until Mann approves a reduction draft.
- No em dashes or middots in UI copy. Numerals for counts. Every count measured at build.
- Fonts only Fraunces, Lora, Outfit. Colours only the tokens above plus the dusk gradient stops already on the live page.
- The phone number appears only in the support line he ruled; nothing else personal.
- `library/` is not deleted.
